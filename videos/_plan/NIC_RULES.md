HOUSE RULES LEARNED FROM THE FINISHED SERIES (apply to lectures 7 and 8; they override anything looser above)
 - Text at least 28 px everywhere. Never dim text below about 0.8 opacity (use a grey tone, or fade the whole group). Keep a 12 px margin inside the 936 x 640 stage.
 - A caption must never run ahead of its picture, and everything a caption claims must be on screen when it is read. Define any jargon on screen before it is used (a small tag, a legend tile).
 - Numbers come from helpers that run the real algorithm and assert the figures at build time; never type a result by hand.
 - update(t) is pure. Seek anywhere and back and the frame must be identical.
 - Recap pictograms play once and then HOLD their finished state (no loop, no blank or reset icon on the last frame); the video ends on a calm complete frame.
 - Keep the whole video at most about 130 s (target 100 to 125 s). Captions use text-wrap: balance (already in videos/video.css: do not edit video.css, engine.js or cards.js).
 - Colour roles stay fixed across the whole video; pick them for the topic in the architect step and write them in the storyboard outline.
